Holocaust Survivors in South Florida

Direct financial assistance and support for Holocaust survivors in need, delivered with our partner Jewish Community Services of South Florida.

South Florida survivors need our help

The Blue Card is a New York–based nonprofit and the only organization in the United States whose sole mission is to provide direct financial assistance to Holocaust survivors in need. We help survivors across the country, including in South Florida, where we work in partnership with Jewish Community Services of South Florida (JCS). Survivors are referred by a social worker or agency. See how to refer a survivor.

Our partnership with JCS

The Blue Card and Jewish Community Services of South Florida work together to reach survivors in need.

Who is JCS?

Jewish Community Services of South Florida (JCS) serves more than 400 Holocaust survivors in Miami-Dade County, according to the Sun Sentinel. Together with The Blue Card, JCS supports about 3,000 survivors living in Florida.

What the partnership funds

The Sun Sentinel reports that funds raised through the partnership help with food assistance, case management and emergency support for survivors.

For social workers and agencies

Holocaust survivors seeking help must be referred by a social service agency or government agency. The Blue Card is an agency of last resort.

  1. Check eligibility. The survivor must have an urgent financial need, no other local or government agency able to help, and live in our service area under Claims Conference guidelines. Call 212-239-2251 or email info@bluecard.org to confirm the area.
  2. Complete the Referral Application. Make a clear recommendation about which program will help most, and include your agency address, phone (with extension) and email.
  3. Mail or email it. Do not fax. Mail to The Blue Card, Attn: The Blue Card Committee, 171 Madison Avenue, Suite 1405, New York, NY 10016, or email info@bluecard.org with the subject line “Application Form for The Blue Card Committee.”
  4. We review and respond. Every application is reviewed carefully and all information is kept confidential.

Questions about South Florida

Does The Blue Card help Holocaust survivors in South Florida?

Yes. The Blue Card helps Holocaust survivors in need across the United States, including South Florida, where it works in partnership with Jewish Community Services of South Florida (JCS). Survivors are referred by a social service or government agency.

Who is Jewish Community Services of South Florida (JCS)?

JCS is a South Florida nonprofit that serves Holocaust survivors in Miami-Dade County. The Sun Sentinel reported in February 2026 that JCS serves more than 400 survivors in Miami-Dade and, together with The Blue Card, supports about 3,000 survivors living in Florida.

How does a South Florida survivor apply for help?

A survivor must be referred by a social service agency or government agency. A case manager reviews the person’s income and expenses, then submits the Referral Application to The Blue Card by mail or email. Please do not fax applications.

Who is eligible?

To be eligible, a survivor must be referred by an agency, have an urgent financial need, have no other local or government agency that can help, and live within The Blue Card’s service area under Claims Conference guidelines. The Blue Card is an agency of last resort. Call 212-239-2251 or email info@bluecard.org to check whether a client lives in the service area.

How can I run the Miami Marathon for Holocaust survivors?

The Blue Card partners with JCS on JCS Team Blue Card for the Miami Marathon and Half Marathon, and runners can take part in person or virtually. See the Team Blue Card page or contact The Blue Card for current details.

Where is The Blue Card located?

The Blue Card’s office is at 171 Madison Avenue, Suite 1405, New York, NY 10016. The phone number is 212-239-2251 and the email is info@bluecard.org. South Florida survivors are served through referrals and the partnership with JCS.

How can I help Holocaust survivors in South Florida?

You can donate, run with JCS Team Blue Card at the Miami Marathon, volunteer in person or virtually, or bring a survivor speaker to your school or organization.
