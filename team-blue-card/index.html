Team Blue Card

What is Team Blue Card?

Team Blue Card is the official endurance fundraising team of The Blue Card. Through marathons, bike tours, and other endurance events, Team Blue Card members raise critical funds and awareness for Holocaust survivors, all over the world. Every mile helps provide essential financial assistance, empowering survivors to live with greater dignity, security, and hope. If you want to get involved, or if you want more information, please email rechan@bluecardfund.org.

FEATURED RACES

The Blue Card is an Official Charity partner once again for the 2026 TCS New York City Marathon! The TCS New York City Marathon is one of the biggest marathons in the world, 26.2 miles that courses through the 5 boroughs of NYC, and hosts runners from over 100 countries. Team Blue Card has been an Official Charity Partner since 2009. Team runners use their training, platform, and the event to raise much needed funds and awareness to help The Blue Card with our mission of helping Holocaust survivors in need. Join Team Blue Card and make this amazing marathon and experience even more special by dedicating it to helping Holocaust survivors!
