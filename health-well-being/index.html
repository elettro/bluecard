Health and Well-being

The Blue Card’s Health and Wellbeing Programs are designed to improve emotional, mental, and physical wellbeing for Holocaust survivors. These programs help improve quality of life in meaningful ways, both large and small.

Telephone Response System

TERS (Telephone Response System) One button. Immediate help. 24/7 protection for Holocaust survivors living independently.

The Blue Card’s TERS program has provided a life-saving solution that restores confidence, safety, and dignity to Holocaust survivors for over a decade.

It is a life-preserving button that can be used inside or outside the home so that Holocaust survivors feel safe and confident in their daily lives. With just a press of a button, the survivor is instantly connected to a care specialist who will contact help if needed, providing 24/7 medical assistance and peace of mind to both survivors and their loved ones.

The Blue Card provides TERS systems at no cost to survivors who cannot afford installation, service, or maintenance.

Siggi B. Wilzig Fighting Cancer Together Program

The Siggi B. Wilzig, OBM, Fighting Cancer Together Program seeks to ensure survivors suffering from cancer have access to the aid they need in order to battle this disease with greater support and less financial strain.

The Siggi B. Wilzig Fighting Cancer Together Program helps survivors with:

Treatment-related out-of-pocket costs.
Transportation support for travel expenses related to cancer care.
Dietary costs and nutrition-related expenses that arise during treatment.
