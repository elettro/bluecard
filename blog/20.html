Fear Of Forgetting: What Happens When Last Holocaust Survivors Are Gone?

← Back to Blog 2025/05/14 at 2:07 pm

From AFP News By Claire GALLEN April 13, 2025, 9:02 pm EDT

Exerpt: “I hope that they believe that we didn’t make up the stories,” said the 100-year-old Canadian, who worries what will happen when the last living witnesses of what was done by the Nazi are gone.

“We are still here, and they (the Holocaust deniers) are already saying it never happened,” he told AFP.

His fears are not unfounded. A UNESCO study in 2022 found that a fifth of content on Twitter about the Holocaust either denied or distorted it. Tiktok was almost as bad with eight percent of content on Facebook also questionable.
