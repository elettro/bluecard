The Blue Card / How We Help / Emergency Cash Assistance

Immediate support during urgent moments

The Blue Card provides one-time emergency grant assistance to help improve the living conditions of Holocaust survivors facing urgent hardship.

For survivors living on limited income, one unexpected expense can create a serious crisis. This assistance helps address essential needs quickly so survivors can remain safer, healthier, and more stable in their homes.

🏠
Housing stability
Support for urgent housing-related needs that threaten safety or shelter.
⚕️
Health protection
Help with medical and dental needs that cannot wait.
🍽️
Basic necessities
Support for food, clothing, and essential daily living items.
🔧
Critical repairs
Help with urgent home or appliance issues that affect daily life.

Emergency needs may include

  • Late payments to help prevent eviction
  • Homelessness prevention
  • Shut-off prevention for utilities
  • Emergency relocation
  • Home add-ins and repairs such as stair lifts or plumbing
  • Purchase and repair of essential appliances
  • Food purchases
  • Dental care
  • Medical care and medical equipment not covered by insurance
  • Essential clothing and footwear
  • Burial expenses

Why this support matters

Many Holocaust survivors continue to live with the emotional weight of displacement, loss, and insecurity. A threat to housing, health, or basic necessities can carry a deep emotional impact in addition to a financial one.

1
Protects daily life
Helps address urgent disruptions before they become larger crises.
2
Supports independence
Helps survivors remain in their homes with greater safety and stability.
3
Preserves dignity
Provides practical help during difficult moments with respect and care.
