The Crisis of Isolation

Many Survivors Are Alone

For many Holocaust survivors, the golden years are often shadowed by intense isolation. Without family or regular visitors, the silence of a home can amplify past traumas.

Anxiety, PTSD, and the feeling of being forgotten are daily struggles. Your digital presence can be the bridge that breaks this silence and restores a sense of community.
