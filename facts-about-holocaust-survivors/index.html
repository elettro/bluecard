Facts About Holocaust Survivors in the United States

Fewer than 200,000 Jewish Holocaust survivors are alive today, and about 31,000 of them live in the United States. The median age is 87. Here are the numbers, with sources.

Last updated November 2026.

How many survivors are alive today?

Population figures come from the Claims Conference on Jewish Material Claims Against Germany (January 2026 data), the most widely cited source for survivor demographics.

About 196,600

Jewish Holocaust survivors alive worldwide

Estimated as of January 2026, down from 220,000 a year earlier. Source: Claims Conference.

About 31,000

survivors live in the United States

That is 16% of all survivors worldwide. Only Israel (97,600, or 50%) has more. Source: Claims Conference.

Median age 87

for survivors worldwide

Survivors range in age from 79 to over 100, and 30% are 90 or older. Source: Claims Conference.

97%

are “child survivors”

Nearly all living survivors were children when the war began and were born in 1928 or later. Source: Claims Conference.

62%

of survivors are women

Only 38% are men. Source: Claims Conference.
