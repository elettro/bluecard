Ongoing Monthly Assistance

Not every need is sudden. For many survivors who live on a fixed and limited income, hardship is ongoing. We provide recurring financial support for Holocaust survivors who need extra help each month to afford basic necessities and services. Ongoing monthly assistance helps reduce constant financial stress and supports greater stability from month to month.

Ongoing support may also include:

  • Food and essential supplies.
  • Basic necessities
  • Housing support
  • Everyday items needed for health, routine, and daily living.

Other ongoing support includes:

  • Jewish Holiday grants for Passover, High Holidays, and Hanukkah
  • Mazel Tov Birthday program — a card and check for every survivor
  • Summer Vacation program for survivors in the New York area
