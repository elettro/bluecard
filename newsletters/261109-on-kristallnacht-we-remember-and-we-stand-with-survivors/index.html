On Kristallnacht, We Remember and We Stand With Survivors

← Back to Newsletters November 9, 2026

From Masha Pearl. Originally sent to The Blue Card email community on November 9, 2026.

Dear Friends,

Eighty-eight years ago tonight, Jewish families across Germany and Austria watched their homes, businesses, and synagogues come under attack. Kristallnacht marked a terrifying turning point, shattering lives and destroying the sense of security that so many families had known.

For the survivors of the Holocaust, the meaning of home and safety would never be quite the same.

I think of Herta, a Holocaust survivor who built a new life in the United States. Decades later, she and her husband faced serious financial difficulties. After everything Herta had endured, the thought of losing her independence was another hardship she should not have had to face.

The Blue Card stepped in, helping with housing expenses, food, medical care, and home assistance. We even helped provide a chairlift so she could continue living independently in her own home.

Herta's story reminds us that remembering the Holocaust also means caring for those who survived it.

Today, The Blue Card supports more than 3,000 Holocaust survivor households across 35 states. Each has a story of extraordinary resilience. Each deserves to live their remaining years with dignity, comfort, and the knowledge that they have not been forgotten.

On this solemn anniversary, I ask you to join us in honoring the past by caring for the survivors who are still with us.
