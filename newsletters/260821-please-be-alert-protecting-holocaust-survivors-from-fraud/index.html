Please Be Alert: Protecting Holocaust Survivors From Fraud

← Back to Newsletters August 21, 2026

From Masha Pearl. Originally sent to The Blue Card email community on August 21, 2026.

Dear Friends,

Over the past several days, The Blue Card has been advising Holocaust survivors, their families, caregivers, and social workers to be on alert for suspicious outreach involving Holocaust compensation claims.

An unverified entity calling itself the “Jewish Holocaust Claims Center” or “JHCC” has been sending unsolicited correspondence encouraging survivors and their families to submit claims for compensation. Survivors should not respond, provide personal or financial information, or make any payment until the organization contacting them has been independently verified.

Below is a redacted example of the correspondence that prompted this warning. Its formal appearance, references to legitimate institutions, and request for a prompt response demonstrate why survivors and families should independently verify any unexpected outreach before taking action.

Yesterday, I spoke with 1010 WINS about this warning, how families can protect the survivors in their lives, and why it is so important to approach anyone who may have responded with compassion rather than embarrassment or judgment.

Listen to my 1010 WINS interview here

If you are a Holocaust survivor, family member, caregiver, or social worker and receive a suspicious communication, please contact The Blue Card at 212-239-2251 or info@bluecard.org. Our team can help guide you toward trusted resources and determine whether additional assistance is needed. Legitimate claims assistance is also available free of charge through the Holocaust Claims Processing Office at 800-695-3318.

The Blue Card serves nearly 3,000 survivor households across the country. Every survivor we serve is over the age of 80, and nearly 70 percent live alone. For someone already struggling to afford food, rent, medication, or medical care, even a small financial loss can quickly become a serious crisis.

This is why The Blue Card’s work is so urgent. We provide direct financial and emergency assistance to Holocaust survivors in need, while ensuring that they have a trusted place to turn when they are frightened, uncertain, or facing an unexpected hardship.

Your support allows us to continue responding when survivors need us most. Please consider making a gift today so that no Holocaust survivor has to face financial hardship or a suspicious situation alone.
