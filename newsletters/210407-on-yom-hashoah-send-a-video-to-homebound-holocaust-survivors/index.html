On Yom HaShoah: send a video to homebound Holocaust survivors

← Back to Newsletters April 7, 2021

Originally sent to The Blue Card email community on April 7, 2021.

Bnei Mitzvah alumn, Suzanna, and Holocaust survivor Dasha Rittenberg Z"L.

On Yom HaShoah, we remember the six million Jews and innocents who lost their lives in the Holocaust because of the hatred of a few. We also use this day to honor the Holocaust survivors who survived, in every meaning of the word; living and pushing past mass tragedy and sharing the memories of their lives and of those who perished.

For the past year and ongoing, many Holocaust survivors have been severely impacted by Covid-19, either mentally, physically, emotionally, and financially. Survivors are the most at-risk group of individuals and so they go to extreme lengths to stay safe, which threatens them mentally by bringing up past trauma, PTSD, and other mental illnesses.

You can help these Holocaust survivors feel better and know that they have not been forgotten by the world by sending them a personal 'hello!'. We are collecting videos tributes to send to homebound Holocaust survivors that can be about anything:

  • read them a moving quote
  • a passage from a book that speaks to you
  • sing a song
  • introduce yourself
  • introduce your family and/or pets
  • anything that will brighten their day!

We hope to receive many videos by the end of the month to send to homebound Holocaust survivors, so please tell your family, friends, neighbors, classmates, and anyone else to submit a video!
