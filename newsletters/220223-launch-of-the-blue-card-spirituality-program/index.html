Launch of The Blue Card Spirituality Program

← Back to Newsletters February 23, 2022

Originally sent to The Blue Card email community on February 23, 2022.

Dear Friend,

We are thrilled to announce that we have launched The Blue Card Spirituality Program – a program that aims to provide emotional and spiritual support to Holocaust survivors through a number of different channels.

As part of the program, we are now offering Weekly Torah Portion Classes.

Every Thursday at 1:00PM EST, survivors can call our hotline which will offer the class in English, followed by a class in Russian at 2:00PM EST

The first class will launch this coming Thursday, February 24, 2022 at 1PM EST . Please dial into the following conference call number: (351) 888-6042. If you wish to hear this in Russian please dial into the same line at 2PM EST.

This is an exciting opportunity for survivors to participate in a program that they otherwise would not be able to.

Please kindly share this information with our mutual clients, as well as with with survivors in your community, their families and your colleagues.

Sincerely,

Masha Pearl

Executive Director
