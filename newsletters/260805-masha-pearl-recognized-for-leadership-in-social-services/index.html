Support Survivors

Dear Friends,

We are proud to share that our Executive Director, Masha Pearl, has been named to City & State NY's 2026 Above & Beyond: Social Services list, which recognizes notable New Yorkers caring for those in need.

As the granddaughter of Holocaust survivors, Masha has dedicated her career to ensuring that the remaining survivors in America can age with dignity. Under her leadership, The Blue Card has expanded to serve thousands of survivor households across 35 states, grown its operating budget by 40%, and launched an innovative Holocaust education program that uses interactive holograms of survivors to bring their testimony directly into classrooms.

In her own words: "Every day, I help ensure that Holocaust survivors who endured unimaginable hardship can age with dignity and know they have not been forgotten. Time is running out, and this work cannot wait."

You can read her full profile here

This recognition belongs to our entire community: the survivors we serve, the donors who make this work possible, the staff who carry it out every day and the volunteers who show up when it matters most.

As survivors age, their needs only grow. Medical costs rise, mobility declines and isolation deepens. Your continued support ensures that Holocaust survivors receive the direct financial assistance they need for food, rent, medical care and prescriptions. To make a gift, please visit bluecard.org/donate.

With gratitude,

The Blue Card
