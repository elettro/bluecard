Dear Friends,

I hope this message finds you and your loved ones safe. As extreme weather continues to impact regions across the country, we are constantly reaching out to Holocaust survivors to ensure they have evacuation plans in place and are safe. However, with the significant damage from Hurricane Helene and Hurricane Milton now threatening further devastation, survivors are facing unprecedented challenges requiring urgent financial assistance.

Holocaust survivors in areas like Asheville, Tampa Bay, and across southern Georgia are in critical need of food, medical supplies, transportation, and caregiver support. These hurricanes have worsened their already difficult conditions, leaving them desperate for essential resources.

As a national organization, we are uniquely positioned to provide immediate assistance. Survivors like Eda, who lives alone in Black Mountain, NC, with no family nearby, faced the hardship of losing power for two weeks. Thankfully, with the support of The Blue Card, she received emergency aid to replenish her groceries and household supplies, providing her with the essentials she needed to get through this challenging time.

In addition to our efforts, we are working with local volunteers and synagogues to mobilize additional support for survivors in need. However, many more survivors remain in urgent need of similar assistance. If you know of any Holocaust survivors in the path of these hurricanes who may need assistance, we encourage you to let us know so we can provide the support they require during these difficult times.

We are reaching out to you in these critical times. Your contributions to our Natural Disasters and Severe Weather Fund will ensure that Holocaust survivors receive life-saving support, from emergency transportation to vital medical care.

Your generosity has always been a pillar of strength for The Blue Card and the survivors we serve. As we respond to the growing impacts of these disasters, your support is needed more than ever.

With heartfelt gratitude,
