Honoring Holocaust Survivors on International Holocaust Remembrance Day

← Back to Newsletters January 27, 2025

From The Blue Card. Originally sent to The Blue Card email community on January 27, 2025.

Today, January 27, marks the 80th commemoration of the liberation of Auschwitz. On this International Holocaust Remembrance Day, we honor the six million Jews who perished.

At The Blue Card, we provide vital financial, medical, and emotional support to Holocaust survivors across the U.S. who struggle daily in order to meet their basic needs of housing, food security, and healthcare. Thanks to your generosity, we can ensure they receive the dignity and care they deserve.

This year New York City Mayor Eric Adams has officially proclaimed January 27th as “The Blue Card Holocaust Survivor Day”—a recognition that underscores the urgent needs of survivors and reaffirms our collective commitment to support those survivors still with us and to stand up against antisemitism.

Miriam S.

Among those we are helping is Miriam S. (name changed for confidentiality), who receives ongoing financial assistance and is also a proud member of The Blue Card Speakers Bureau.

Your support makes a world of difference. Thank you for all you do for these brave individuals.

With gratitude,
