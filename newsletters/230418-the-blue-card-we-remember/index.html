Yom HaShoah - We Remember

Yom HaShoah – a solemn day of commemoration – we honor the memory of the six million who perished in the Holocaust. It is our duty to pass on their memories, their stories and to vow to never forget. As more years pass since the end of the war, it becomes more critical to remember so the world does not forget the atrocities of the Holocaust.

It is also a time to recommit to ensuring that survivors are cared for. They carry the trauma of the Holocaust every day of their lives, and as they age, many are being forgotten.

The Blue Card helps Holocaust survivors, in a meaningful and concrete way, that recognizes their unique needs. Meet one of those survivors, Leah.

She was a young girl in Hungary in 1944 when the Nazis sent her family to a concentration camp in Austria, where they survived for a year before being sent on a death march as Allied forces closed in. They escaped with the improbable help of a Nazi soldier, who took pity after initially mistaking the blond-haired Leah for his own daughter and instructing them to remove their Jewish stars of David and flee to the mountains where they remained in hiding until the end of the war.

Today, Leah is 89 years old and lives alone in Brooklyn. She could not get by without The Blue Card and is thankful for its ongoing financial support.
