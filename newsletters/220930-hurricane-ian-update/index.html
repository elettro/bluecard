HURRICANE IAN UPDATE

Dear Friends of The Blue Card,

We are reaching out with an update about Hurricane Ian. As a national nonprofit, we serve Holocaust survivors across the country but have an especially large survivor population in Florida. In response to the devastation of Hurricane Ian, The Blue Card is working with agencies on the ground to check on all Holocaust survivors we serve and provide assistance in any way we can.

We have all heard the news of Hurricane Ian's destruction and have seen the images of flooding, downed power lines, and damaged homes. The Blue Card is committed to helping survivors affected by the storm rebuild and re-establish their safety as soon as possible.

Holocaust survivors are especially vulnerable when a natural disaster occurs, as they may be physically unable to leave their homes, may not have the funds to be able to evacuate, and may experience PTSD symptoms as a result of severe weather conditions and isolation.

Please consider making a donation to support Holocaust survivors in Florida today.
