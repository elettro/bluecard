Today is the 85th Anniversary of Kristallnacht,

“Night of Broken Glass”

On November 9-10,1938, a series of pogroms against the Jewish population in which synagogues were razed, businesses vandalized, books burned, and men kidnapped from their homes.

Jews across Germany and German-occupied territories woke up to state-sponsored violence against them. 85 years ago today was the night of pogroms now known as Kristallnacht, or The Night of Broken Glass, seen by many as a precursor to the Holocaust.

On the night of November 9th, 1938, Inge Auerbacher, pictured above, was four years old. She still remembers the smashing of every window in her family's house with rocks. She hid in the back shed with her mother and grandmother all night to escape the violence, but her father and grandfather were arrested with all the other Jewish men in the city and were taken to Dachau for weeks.

Kristallnacht was just the beginning.
