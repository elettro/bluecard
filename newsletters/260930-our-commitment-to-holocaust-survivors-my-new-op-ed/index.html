Our Commitment to Holocaust Survivors - My New Op-Ed

← Back to Newsletters September 30, 2026

From Masha Pearl. Originally sent to The Blue Card email community on September 30, 2026.

Dear Friends,

At The Blue Card, our commitment to Holocaust survivors is at the heart of everything we do. We want every survivor to know that when they need help, they have somewhere to turn and a community that cares.

I recently wrote an op-ed for eJewishPhilanthropy, “Every Holocaust Remembrance Budget Should Help a Living Survivor,” about something I believe deserves greater attention: caring for living survivors should be part of every major effort to preserve Holocaust memory.

In the op-ed, I propose a “Living Memory Standard.” When we invest in Holocaust education, exhibitions, memorials, or projects to preserve testimony, we should also consider how those efforts can help survivors today.

That could mean pairing an exhibition with a campaign for survivor assistance or connecting an educational initiative with an organization providing direct care.

For survivors, that care can mean groceries, transportation to a medical appointment, help with medical expenses, or repairs that allow them to remain safely and comfortably in their own homes.

Preserving Holocaust memory and caring for Holocaust survivors should go hand in hand.

I hope you will read my op-ed and share it with your friends, colleagues, and community. This is a conversation I hope more of us will have—and a commitment I hope more institutions will make.

You can also put that commitment into practice today by making a gift to The Blue Card. Your generosity helps us provide food, cover medical expenses, assist with housing needs, and respond when a survivor faces an emergency.

Most importantly, your support reminds survivors that they are cared for and that they can count on their community.

Thank you for being part of The Blue Card community and for helping us care for Holocaust survivors with the dignity and compassion they deserve.

Warmly,

Masha Pearl

Executive Director

The Blue Card
