From Masha Pearl. Originally sent to The Blue Card email community on April 6, 2026.

Continue to Support Survivors This Passover

Dear Friends,

Last week, The Blue Card had the opportunity to partner with B’nai B’rith on a meaningful event.

Across New York, Holocaust survivors are getting through this holiday with the food and support they need. That is not something we ever take for granted.

In a recent Fox 5 segment, which aired at 5 p.m. and 10 p.m. on Fox 5 Television, streamed on the Fox Local App and the Fox 5 NY YouTube channel, Holocaust survivor Noemi Kohn (a Blue Card recipient) shared her story. Young Jewish New Yorkers sat with her, listened, and then packed Passover food packages for survivors across our community. Those packages are reaching individuals who might otherwise struggle to afford even the most basic essentials for the holiday.

We are proud to be part of that effort, and we are especially grateful to B’nai B’rith for their partnership in helping make this possible.

At The Blue Card, we know these survivors. We are in their homes, on the phone with them, helping them navigate daily challenges. And through partnerships like this, we help ensure that food and support reach them when it matters most.

We are grateful to Noemi Kohn for sharing her story and helping connect a new generation to the lived experiences of Holocaust survivors. That connection is what makes this work so meaningful.

This is not about one organization leading. It is about a community coming together to care for those who came before us.

Check it out directly on Fox 5 or on YouTube.
