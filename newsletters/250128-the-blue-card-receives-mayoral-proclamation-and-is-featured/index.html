The Blue Card Receives Mayoral Proclamation and is Featured on NY1

← Back to Newsletters January 28, 2025

From The Blue Card. Originally sent to The Blue Card email community on January 28, 2025.

Dear Friends,

As we reflect on Holocaust Remembrance Day, we are honored to share some significant achievements that underscore our ongoing commitment to supporting Holocaust survivors.

I am pleased to highlight some remarkable developments made possible by your unwavering support and dedication to the survivors:

The Blue Card Holocaust Survivor Day Proclamation: On January 27, New York City Mayor Eric Adams officially proclaimed the day as The Blue Card Holocaust Survivor Day. This designation serves not only to honor the survivors but also to reinforce the importance of our mission to meet their needs with dignity and care. Receiving this recognition strengthens our resolve to continue advocating for and assisting the last generation of Holocaust survivors.

Menashe Shapiro, Deputy Chief of Staff and Senior Advisor to the Mayor, presenting Mayoral Proclamation to Masha Pearl, Executive Director of The Blue Card

Spotlight on NY1: Amplifying the Voices of Survivors

In recognition of Holocaust Remembrance Day, The Blue Card was featured on NY1, shining a light on our vital work in supporting Holocaust survivors. This media coverage plays a crucial role in raising awareness and ensuring more individuals understand the urgent need for financial, medical, and emotional support for those who endured unimaginable hardships.

Watch the clip below.

A Global Conversation: Uniting for Holocaust Survivors:

In honor of International Holocaust Remembrance Day, The Blue Card hosted a powerful webinar with The Survivor Mitzvah Project and Latet, bringing together voices from around the world to address the urgent needs of Holocaust survivors.

Panelists discussed critical challenges survivors face today, including financial hardship, medical care, and aging in isolation, emphasizing our shared responsibility to support them. Survivor testimonies and interactive discussions reinforced the importance of education, advocacy, and collective action in preserving their legacy.

This webinar was a call to action—a reminder that caring for Holocaust survivors is a global commitment, and their voices must never be forgotten.

Thanks to your generosity and dedication, The Blue Card continues to make a meaningful impact. Your continued generosity allows us to provide essential aid—whether through financial assistance, medical care, or emergency support—to ensure survivors live with dignity. As we move forward, we invite you to stay engaged, share our mission, and contribute to sustaining this critical work.

Thank you for being a part of this mission. Together, we honor the past by caring for those who lived it.

With gratitude,

Masha Pearl

Executive Director
