Holocaust Survivors Make, Deliver Hanukkah Care Packages for 20 Freed Gaza Hostages

← Back to Newsletters January 6, 2026

From Masha Pearl. Originally sent to The Blue Card email community on January 6, 2026.

Support Survivors Now

Dear Friend,

I wanted to share something truly moving that was recently featured in The Jerusalem Post. Holocaust survivors supported by The Blue Card came together to prepare Hanukkah care packages for 20 recently freed Gaza hostages. These packages included water, tea, socks, blankets, and handwritten notes of encouragement. Each one was a quiet but powerful expression of compassion, resilience, and Jewish unity during an incredibly painful moment for our people.

As I shared in the article, “Holocaust survivors and former hostages share a uniquely painful bond shaped by survival and resilience.” In a time when fear and uncertainty feel overwhelming, this act of kindness reminded us all of the strength that comes from connection and shared humanity.

The survivors we serve are among the most vulnerable members of our community. Many are elderly, living alone, and struggling to make ends meet. Programs like this one do more than meet basic needs. They restore dignity, purpose, and hope. This is the kind of impact that is only possible because of the compassion of supporters like you.

I am deeply grateful for your partnership and belief in our mission. Because of you, survivors are not only cared for but also empowered to give back in meaningful ways.

By supporting The Blue Card, you help provide Holocaust survivors with care, dignity, and a sense of belonging when it matters most.

Thank you for standing with our survivors and with our community.

With heartfelt gratitude,

Masha Pearl

Executive Director
