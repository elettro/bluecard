Message from Cesare Civetta at the Beethoven Festival Orchestra

I am absolutely thrilled to announce:

A Tribute to Elie Wiesel!!!

Monday, Sept 13th at 5 PM Eastern

Many people may only know Elie Wiesel as the author of Night. And that's why I'm SO excited, because his life's story is absolutely fascinating and a great example and inspiration! Having been a prisoner at both Auschwitz and Buchenwald concentration camps, Wiesel survived the Holocaust and evolved into an incredibly prolific writer and philosopher. He was an indefatigable activist, not only for the Jewish people, but for innumerable causes throughout the world. His work should be kept front and center, especially today. I can't wait until Sunday and Monday!

AND, I will feature him singing three songs in Yiddish: Oyfn Pripetshik, Es Brent, Ven Ich Vol Gehat Koiech, and three in Hebrew: Ani Ma'amin, Shebshifleinu, and Harachaman.

Here is a sneak peak preview: https://youtu.be/OE1dYkbmUc0

Let me know if you will attend. And definitely, please spread the word widely.

Very best wishes,

Cesare Civetta, Founder and Music Director,

Beethoven Festival Orchestra
