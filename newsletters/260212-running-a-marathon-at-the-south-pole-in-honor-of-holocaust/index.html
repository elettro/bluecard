Running a Marathon at the South Pole in Honor of Holocaust Survivors

← Back to Newsletters February 12, 2026

From Masha Pearl. Originally sent to The Blue Card email community on February 12, 2026.

Dr. Isador Lieberman, MD, Proudly Holding The Blue Card Flag at the Antarctica Marathon.

Dear Friends,

Recently, one of our longtime supporters and champions, Dr. Isador Lieberman, did something extraordinary. He traveled to the bottom of the world to run the Antarctica Marathon, one of the most remote and physically demanding races on earth. Dr. Lieberman ran on behalf of The Blue Card in memory of his father, Max Lieberman, a Holocaust survivor, and in honor of survivors everywhere.

Carrying his father’s story across the ice, through extreme cold, isolation, and unforgiving terrain, he reflected on the reality Holocaust survivors endured without preparation, protection, or choice. Survivors like Max survived camps, ghettos, and persecution. Forced to survive brutal winters and blistering summers without food, shelter, medical care, or safety, they carry the physical and emotional scars of that trauma for the rest of their lives.

Today, Holocaust survivors are more vulnerable than ever. In addition to the weight of this trauma, chronic health conditions, and deep isolation, many live far below the poverty line while the cost of living and medical care continue to rise. This is the reality that drives The Blue Card’s work to support survivors every single day.

Dr. Lieberman dedicated his run to raise awareness and support for survivors who do not have a voice and who rely on organizations like The Blue Card to meet their most basic needs. His journey is a powerful reminder that survival is not only about enduring the past, but also about showing up for survivors now.

Dr. Lieberman’s story was recently featured in SpineLine Magazine, highlighting both the extraordinary challenge of the Antarctica Marathon and the mission behind his run. We are honored that The Blue Card and the survivors we serve are part of that story.

Because of supporters like you, survivors are not forgotten. They are supported, they are cared for, and they are treated with dignity today, when it matters most.

To stand with Holocaust survivors and support this critical work, please consider supporting The Blue Card.

Thank you,

Masha Pearl

Executive Director
