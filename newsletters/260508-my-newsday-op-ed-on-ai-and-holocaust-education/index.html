Dear Friends,

I wanted to share a recent op-ed I wrote for Newsday on a topic that has become increasingly urgent: the role of artificial intelligence in Holocaust education.

At The Blue Card, we work every day to preserve the voices and dignity of Holocaust survivors while also ensuring that future generations continue to learn from them. As the survivor population declines, new technologies are emerging that can help bring testimony into classrooms in meaningful ways. But with that opportunity comes responsibility.

In this piece, I discuss the importance of setting ethical boundaries for AI in Holocaust education. The AI-powered survivor hologram program we support was intentionally designed not to invent, interpret, or create responses. Instead, it retrieves only a survivor’s actual recorded words. In Holocaust education, where accuracy and truth are sacred obligations, that distinction matters deeply.

This conversation is not just about technology. It is about memory, trust, and protecting the integrity of survivor testimony for generations to come.

Thank you, as always, for helping us continue this critical work.
