January 27th is International Holocaust Remembrance day.

January 27th we remember the 6 million Jews and the millions of others who perished in the Holocaust.

January 27th we remember the thousands more who were hurt by the Nazi regime and survived this horrible ordeal after loosing everything and everyone.

On January 27, 1945, the Soviet army liberated Auschwitz Concentration Camp, freeing the 7,000 prisoners left behind by the fleeing Nazis. Only days before, around 60,000 prisoners and Jews were forced west in the Death Marches that killed thousands along the way.

The Blue Card vows to Never Forget the 6 million Jews who died as a result of the atrocities and most importantly, ensures that the remaining survivors live out their years with dignity and respect.

Every survivor who experienced the horrors of the Holocaust has their own unique story, and we must honor and remember all of them on International Holocaust Remembrance Day -- and every day.

In commemoration of International Holocaust Remembrance Day, we ask that you honor and remember those who suffered in the Holocaust by supporting The Blue Card's continuing work.
