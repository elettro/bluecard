Dear Friends,

Today, June 4th, is Holocaust Survivor Day. While International Holocaust Remembrance Day and Yom Hashoah solemnly commemorate those lost during the Holocaust, Holocaust Survivor Day turns toward a different kind of remembrance, one rooted in testimony and the enduring power of the human spirit.

Holocaust Survivor Day celebrates those who endured unimaginable suffering and chose, in its aftermath, to rebuild their lives and share their stories. As the survivor generation ages, their voices grow more precious with every passing year.

Take for example, David Eihorn, a Brooklyn-based survivor who will soon be celebrating his 100th birthday this July! David grew up in Sighet, Romania living a peaceful life until being sent to a ghetto and ultimately Auschwitz. After the war, he immigrated to America where he built a life with his late wife, Gita.

Holocaust Survivor Day is meant to celebrate survivors' lives and actively address their current needs - through programming that is proactive, engaging, and truly meaningful.

This is not passive remembrance. It is a call to action. An invitation for communities, organizations, and individuals everywhere to show up for survivors in tangible, personal ways. Your support helps survivors like David and reminds them that they are still valued and not forgotten.

With gratitude,

Masha Pearl
