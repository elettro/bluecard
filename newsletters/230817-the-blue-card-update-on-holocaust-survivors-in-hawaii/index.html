The Blue Card - Update on Holocaust Survivors in Hawaii

← Back to Newsletters August 17, 2023

From The Blue Card. Originally sent to The Blue Card email community on August 17, 2023.

Dear Friends,

In the past week, while wildfires have devastated the island of Maui, it has been our top concern that the Holocaust Survivors of Hawaii are safe and well during these unprecedented times. We would like to update you on the status of these survivors, and after contacting the members of the JCS of Hawaii, we have been assured that both the survivors that The Blue Card knows of and assists, as well as the team members of JCS, are indeed safe, and are doing okay in spite of everything.

While the survivors are okay, they are anticipating an increase in the cost of living expenses. Even though the fires have been contained, they have destroyed thousands of acres of land and property, and very likely there will be a shortage of necessary supplies.

Therefore, we are asking for your help. There are many programs that The Blue Card is helping provide to the survivors of Hawaii, including food, trauma counseling, volunteer services, and long and short-term care for those who may be in need.

Your generous donation will greatly benefit those who may be struggling financially in Hawaii, and would help us get them what they need.

Please donate to The Blue Card's Natural Disasters and Severe Weather Fund.

We appreciate your continued support and commitment to The Blue Card's mission.
