Statement on the Attack on Capitol Hill

← Back to Newsletters January 7, 2021

Originally sent to The Blue Card email community on January 7, 2021.

Dear Blue Card Community,

Yesterday we witnessed an assault not only on the Capitol, but also on America’s democratic institutions.

Regardless of our backgrounds, political affiliations, or personal ideologies, it is incumbent upon all of us to condemn these acts of violence. It is our role to speak out in support of our nation’s democratic values — the same values that inspired Holocaust survivors to settle in this nation committed to the pursuit of justice and truth.

In this challenging time, Holocaust survivors who witnessed violence in Europe against their communities are extremely concerned. Each of us must play a role in restoring civility in our society while maintaining respect for difference of opinion.

Sincerely,

The Blue Card
