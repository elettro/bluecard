Jewish Soldiers & Fighters in WWII Special Programs

Originally sent to The Blue Card email community on November 14, 2021.

Dear Friends,

The Blavatnik Archive is presenting Jewish Soldiers & Fighters in WWII, an international virtual conference on November 14—15, 2021. As part of the Foundation's global effort to remember, celebrate, and recognize Jewish soldiers, Holocaust survivors, and all of their decedents, two special events will take place.

  • Soundscape of the War: Yiddish Songs of the Red Army will take place on Sunday, November 14 at 3:45PM EST. This program will feature Anna Shternshis and Psoy Korolenko, members of the Grammy-nominated Yiddish Glory project. Drawing from a recently discovered archive of Yiddish music created in the Soviet Union during World War II, this lecture-concert will focus on the ways in which Yiddish-speaking soldiers and their family members made sense of the war and the Holocaust as it unfolded.
  • Songs from Testimonies will take place on Monday, November 15 at 4:45PM EST. This program will feature Zisl Slepovich and Sasha Lurje as they draw from the Fortunoff Video Archive for Holocaust Testimonies (Yale University) to present the songs that people sang to depict their lives and experiences in ghettos, in the Red Army, and under German captivity as prisoners of war.

Registration is free and available at JewishSoldiersInWWII.org
