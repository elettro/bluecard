Dear Friends,

As Yom HaShoah comes to a close, we are thinking about the weight of this day, the memories it carries, and the responsibility we all share to ensure that Holocaust survivors are not only remembered, but heard, supported, and cared for.

That is why today was such a meaningful moment for The Blue Card.

Our work was featured on both NBC News and CBS News, giving a broad audience the chance to see not only who we are, but what it looks like to stand with Holocaust survivors in a real and deeply human way.

Both segments highlighted Sip & Listen, our meaningful partnership with the Yad Vashem USA Foundation, which brings Holocaust survivors into neighborhood coffee shops for intimate conversations with younger generations. What made this coverage so powerful was that it was not just about Holocaust remembrance in theory. It was about The Blue Card in action, making sure survivors are supported, seen, heard, and honored.

That is what The Blue Card does every single day.

We are the only national nonprofit in the United States solely dedicated to providing direct financial assistance to Holocaust survivors. We help survivors live with dignity, security, and care. And we work to ensure their voices are never lost.

To see this work recognized by both NBC and CBS was incredibly moving. Viewers witnessed Holocaust survivors sharing their stories in their own words and saw how The Blue Card helps create moments of remembrance, education, and human connection. These conversations are personal, urgent, and increasingly rare.

At a time of rising antisemitism and deepening division, this visibility matters. It matters that survivors were seen and heard. It matters that younger people showed up to listen. And it matters that The Blue Card was there, not just for a single news cycle, but every day, standing with survivors in real and lasting ways.

As this day of remembrance ends, we are proud that these stories reached so many people. More than that, we are proud to do this work every day, quietly and consistently, for survivors who deserve dignity, support, and the knowledge that they will never be forgotten.

Thank you for standing with us and with the survivors whose lives and voices continue to teach all of us what memory, courage, and resilience truly mean.

Thank you,
