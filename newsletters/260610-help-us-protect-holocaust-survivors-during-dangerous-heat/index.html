Dear Friend,

As New York City faces dangerous heat this week, The Blue Card’s Natural Disasters and Severe Weather Emergency Fund is ready to respond. But we need your help to ensure every survivor who needs us receives support.

This fund exists for moments exactly like this. When extreme weather puts Holocaust survivors at risk, we are able to act quickly with immediate, practical assistance that can help keep them safe.

For an elderly survivor living alone, a heat wave is not just uncomfortable. It can mean not having working air conditioning, struggling to pay a higher electric bill, running low on food or medication, or having no one nearby to check in.

Your donation today can help provide:

Bottled water and shelf-stable food

Emergency medication and pharmacy pick-up coordination

Air conditioner and fan repair or replacement

Utility assistance for electric, water, gas, and phone bills

Transportation to cooling centers, community centers, or medical appointments

Wellness visits and check-in calls

Social worker outreach and emotional support for isolated survivors

New York City officials are warning service providers to check on older adults and those most at risk during this heat emergency. For The Blue Card, that work has already begun. Our emergency fund allows us to respond with dignity, urgency, and compassion when survivors need help most.

Each summer, more than 500 New Yorkers die prematurely because of heat-related causes. For Holocaust survivors, many of whom are elderly, live alone, and face serious health challenges, the danger is even greater.

That is why your gift matters right now.

A contribution to The Blue Card’s Natural Disasters and Severe Weather Emergency Fund helps us move quickly before a crisis becomes a tragedy. It helps us deliver food and water, repair or replace cooling equipment, assist with utility bills, arrange transportation, and check in on survivors who may otherwise be alone.

Please make a gift today.

Your support will help protect Holocaust survivors during this heat wave and ensure that The Blue Card can respond again when the next emergency comes.

With gratitude,

Masha Pearl

Executive Director
