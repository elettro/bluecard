Dear Friends,

Yesterday, our Executive Director, Masha Pearl, published a deeply moving op-ed in the Miami Herald that captures what we see every single day at The Blue Card. She wrote with urgency because the needs are urgent. She wrote with honesty because survivors deserve nothing less. And she wrote because time is running out.

In her piece, Masha describes what many people choose to overlook. Survivors who rebuilt their lives after the Holocaust are now in their nineties and older. They are frail, alone, and living on fixed incomes that simply do not cover their basic needs. Many struggle to afford medication. Many skip meals to stretch their budgets. Some are choosing between utilities and food. These are not abstractions. These are real conversations, real cries for help, and real human beings who have already endured more than anyone should.

The Blue Card exists for this moment. And this moment cannot wait.

Masha’s op-ed is not just a reflection. It is a call to responsibility. Survivors did everything society asked of them. They rebuilt families. They built businesses. They paid taxes. They shaped communities. And now, in the final chapter of their lives, they are depending on people like us to stand with them.

This is where you come in.

Your support directly changes the outcome of their day. It keeps their lights on. It pays for home health aides when they can no longer stand. It delivers meals when they cannot get out of bed. It covers rent shortfalls so they are not evicted. It pays for medical care when insurance stops covering it. It is the difference between someone worrying on the floor of a dark apartment and someone answering their door to a bag of fresh food.

This is not charity. It is dignity.

I ask you to read Masha’s article today. And after reading it, I ask you to do something. Make a gift. Increase your support. Please share it with someone who cares. Please forward it to family or friends who believe that survivors matter.

Every gift matters. Every act matters. The window of time we have to make a meaningful difference is closing fast.

Masha spoke with courage and urgency. Now we need to answer with action.

Thank you for standing with us. Thank you for showing survivors that they are not forgotten.

With deep appreciation,

The Blue Card Team
