Dear Friends,

The Blue Card is thrilled to announce our partnership with Cost Plus Drug Company!

This partnership will enable The Blue Card to offer Holocaust Survivors nationwide access to low-cost generic medication. The Blue Card will cover the cost of all medication which will be shipped directly to their homes.

All Holocaust survivors can benefit from this program. The Blue Card will be offering educational seminars on medication affordability.

To learn more about the program, please click on the following link:

https://bluecard.org/cost-plus-drug-company/

Entrepreneur Mark Cuban founded Cost Plus Drug Company in 2016 to provide Americans with low-cost versions of high-cost generic drugs. Through their wholesale-direct to pharmacy model, the Mark Cuban Plus Drug Company radically reduces the price of generics which directly passes the savings to the consumer.
