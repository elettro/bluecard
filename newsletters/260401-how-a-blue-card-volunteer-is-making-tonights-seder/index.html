How a Blue Card Volunteer is Making Tonight’s Seder Different for Holocaust Survivors

Support Survivors This Passover

Griffin Tenenbaum (Photo: The Blue Card)

Dear Friends,

Tonight is the Seder.

Across our community, families will gather around the table to retell our story, celebrate freedom, and be together.

But for many Holocaust survivors, this night can feel very different.

Some are alone. Others are worried about how they will afford even the most basic essentials for the holiday.

And yet, because of people like you of all ages and locations, something else is also happening.

As highlighted in a recent Long Island Times article, Griffin, a Long Island teen, partnered with Stop & Shop to assemble Passover care packages for Holocaust survivors, filled with matzah, grape juice, and other holiday essentials. It is a powerful reminder that people of all ages are stepping up to ensure survivors are seen, supported, and never forgotten.

As Griffin shared, “Since I was five years old, Camp Kinder Ring has shaped my connection to Holocaust survivors, especially through mentors like Mikhl Baran, whose story and message of kindness left a lasting impact on me. For my Bar Mitzvah, I chose to support Holocaust survivors, which led me to The Blue Card. Over the past three years, I’ve continued volunteering, organizing fundraisers and care packages, inspired by the strength of survivors and the importance of being an upstander.”

That same spirit is what makes our work possible.

Through The Blue Card, more than 3,000 Holocaust survivor households are receiving critical support, including food, medical care, and emergency assistance, so they can approach this holiday with dignity.

As we sit down tonight, we are reminded that freedom is not just something we celebrate. It is something we are responsible for protecting for others.

Before the holiday begins, I am asking you to take one more step.

Please consider making a generous Passover gift today to ensure that no Holocaust survivor is left without the care and support they need, not just tonight, but in the days and months ahead.

You can make your gift here.

Thank you for making sure Holocaust survivors are not alone tonight.

Wishing you and your family a wonderful Passover.

With gratitude,

Masha Pearl

Executive Director
