The Blue Card / How We Help / Dental and Medical Assistance

Focused help for essential care

The Blue Card provides one-time financial assistance for urgent dental and medical needs that directly impact health and daily function.

Many Holocaust survivors live on fixed incomes and face health needs that are difficult to afford. When important services fall outside insurance coverage or create added financial strain, survivors may delay care or go without it.

This program helps reduce that burden by supporting urgent needs that directly affect comfort, health, and daily functioning.

Dental and denture care
Help with urgent dental services and denture-related needs.
Hearing aids
Support for essential hearing assistance.
Medical expenses
Support for care, medication, or equipment not covered by insurance.

Assistance may include

  • Dental care
  • Denture care
  • Hearing aids
  • Medical expenses not otherwise covered by insurance
  • Medication support
  • Medical equipment support

Why this matters

Access to care directly impacts health, independence, communication, comfort, and quality of life. Timely support helps survivors address urgent needs before those needs become larger problems.
