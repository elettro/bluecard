Medication Assistance

Studies have shown that Holocaust survivors are not only more likely to face serious chronic health challenges, but often carry a lifetime of physical and emotional burden tied to profound trauma. As the Holocaust survivor population ages, health and medication needs can become more common, AND more difficult to access and afford. The Blue Card’s programs aim to relieve some of those concerns.

Medical and dental care not covered by insurance

The Blue Card provides one-time financial assistance for urgent dental and medical needs that directly impact health and daily function, before those needs become larger problems.

Many Holocaust survivors live on fixed incomes and can face unexpected health needs that are difficult to afford. When important services fall outside insurance coverage or create added financial strain, survivors may delay care or go without it.

This program helps reduce that burden by supporting urgent needs that directly affect comfort, health, and daily functioning.

Emergency Medical and Dental Assistance may include:

  • Dental care
  • Denture care
  • Hearing aids
  • Medical expenses not otherwise covered by insurance
  • Medication support
  • Medical equipment support

The Blue Card's Medication Assistance Grant

The Blue Card Medication Assistance Grant provides financial assistance to help Holocaust survivors afford lifesaving medication and prescription co-pays so they do not have to choose between their health and their basic daily needs.

According to a recent study, 18 million Americans were recently unable to pay for at least one prescription medication for their household due to rising costs, and 1 in 10 Americans have skipped doses to save money. Holocaust survivors are too often part of this crisis. No survivor should have to ration medication because the cost is out of reach. The Blue Card helps remove that burden and restore stability, dignity, and peace of mind.

Cost Plus Drug Company Partnership

This partnership helps Holocaust survivors nationwide access low-cost generic medication delivered directly to their homes, expanding access to affordable medication.

All Holocaust survivors can benefit from this program. It helps create another pathway to lower-cost medication and clearer information around access. The Blue Card also offers educational seminars on medication affordability to help survivors understand available options more clearly.
