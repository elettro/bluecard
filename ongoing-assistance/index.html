Program Summary

What this program provides

This program provides direct support services from The Blue Card to address urgent needs and improve day-to-day stability.

Contact The Blue Card or use the application pathways on this site to request support, confirm eligibility, and begin next steps.

The Blue Card / How We Help / Ongoing Monthly Assistance

Steady help for everyday needs

For Holocaust survivors who need extra assistance each month so they have access to basic necessities and services, The Blue Card provides monthly financial support.

Many survivors live on fixed and limited income. Ongoing monthly assistance helps reduce constant financial stress and supports greater stability from month to month.

Food and supplies purchases
Support for everyday items needed for health, routine, and daily living.
Rent assistance
Help with housing costs that are essential to maintaining stability at home.

Why monthly support matters

Not every need is sudden. For many survivors, hardship is ongoing. Monthly assistance helps create a more reliable foundation for daily life.

🛒Basic necessities
Helps cover food and essential supplies.
🏠Housing support
Helps reduce pressure around rent and staying at home.
📅Month-to-month stability
Supports greater consistency and less financial strain.
