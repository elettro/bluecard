Helping survivors get the medication they need

The Blue Card’s Medication Assistance Grant provides financial assistance for survivors to aid them in purchasing lifesaving medication and covering co-pays.

According to a recent study, 18 million Americans were recently unable to pay for at least one prescription medication for their household due to rising costs, and 1 in 10 Americans have skipped doses to save money.

Holocaust survivors are too often part of this crisis. Many live on limited monthly incomes and pensions, forcing impossible decisions about how to stretch every dollar.

The support survivors receive from The Blue Card is invaluable. It allows them to receive the medication they need without added financial distress, and with genuine relief and transparency.
