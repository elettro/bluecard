Meet Sonia

Sonia Warshawski’s story remains at the center of the experience.

Sonia Warshawski, nee Grynstein, was born November 10, 1925, in Miezric, Poland. She was 13 when the war broke out, and 15 when Nazis rounded up the Jews in the town and forced them into the Miezric Ghetto.

Sonia was imprisoned at Auschwitz, Majdanek, where she saw her mother being marched to the gas chamber, and Bergen-Belsen, where she was liberated by British troops at 19. She immigrated to the United States in 1948 and settled in Kansas City, Kansas.

Decades later, Sonia found her true purpose in human connection. From her tiny tailor shop in the basement of a fading shopping mall, she told her story of survival to anyone who would listen. Her public presence grew into classrooms, churches, synagogues, packed theaters, museums, and even beyond prison walls.

Through presentations, personal connections, and the Big Sonia documentary, her joys and immeasurable wounds are revealed. The film has inspired and educated audiences across the United States and abroad.

The Blue Card is honored to continue sharing Sonia’s story through this program.

Recommended Session Flow

A flexible program built around your audience.

The Blue Card has seen strong results with two-hour sessions. Longer and shorter sessions are available. The program is flexible for every teacher, school, student group, and audience.

Recommended minimum: two hours

Two hours gives students time to understand Sonia’s background, watch the film, reflect, ask questions through the hologram, and complete pre-viewing and post-viewing surveys. This recommendation is helpful, not required.

Plan Your Session
1

Introduction

Students are introduced to the Holocaust, Sonia, and The Blue Card.

2

Pre-viewing survey

Students complete a brief survey so The Blue Card can understand their Holocaust knowledge before the presentation.

3

Big Sonia documentary

Students watch the 45-minute documentary to understand Sonia’s Holocaust history and who she is as a person.

4

Guided reflection

A moderator from The Blue Card helps facilitate a brief reflection on the film.

5

Q&A with Sonia

Students engage with Sonia through the interactive hologram experience.

6

Post-viewing survey

Students complete a final survey to help measure what they learned and how the program impacted them.
