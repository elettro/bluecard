The Blue Card / How We Help / Health and Wellbeing Programs

Supporting the whole person

The Blue Card’s Health and Wellbeing Programs are designed to improve emotional, mental, and physical wellbeing for Holocaust survivors. These programs address isolation, seasonal hardship, nutrition, recognition, and quality of life.

🕎

Jewish Holiday Support

Financial support for the High Holidays, Hanukkah, and Passover to help survivors afford extra items for the holidays.

🎂

Mazal Tov Birthday

Birthday cards and birthday money for survivors who often lost their entire families during the war.

☀️

Mood Restore Light Box

A compact, portable, UV-free light therapy unit that supports seasonal wellbeing and daily balance.

🐾

Companion Pets

Interactive companion cats and dogs that provide comfort, connection, and emotional support.

🌳

Summer Vacation

A few days away from the city with accessible accommodations, transportation, meals, and activities.

💊

Vitamins

Multivitamins, minerals, supplements, liquid meals, and other items that help support daily health.

🙂

Bring a Smile

Additional support for terminally ill Holocaust survivors, helping grant a survivor’s final wish.

💙

Responsive support

During difficult periods such as the pandemic, these programs helped lift spirits and reduce isolation.

These programs help improve quality of life in meaningful ways, both large and small.
