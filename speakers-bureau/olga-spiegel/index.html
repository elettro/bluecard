About Olga

Born on 06/05/1943 in France, lives in NYC, Manhattan, artist.

In 1943 she fled with her mother across border to Italy, while her father was taken to concentration camp. She hid in a stable for many months and eventually made her way to Rome. In Rome, a priest hid her in a back room where she stayed until a liberation.
