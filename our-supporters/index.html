Our Supporters

The Blue Card is deeply grateful to all of our partners, sponsors, and grantors. Their support strengthens our mission and helps deliver vital assistance, compassionate care, and meaningful outreach to Holocaust survivors in need.

This includes:

  • Support for direct assistance and life-sustaining services
  • Investment in education, outreach, and community response
  • Partnerships that extend compassion to vulnerable older adults

We recognize the foundations, agencies, community organizations, and philanthropic leaders who form a trusted network that stand with The Blue Card in supporting Holocaust survivors:
