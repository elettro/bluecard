With sincere gratitude

We recognize the foundations, agencies, community organizations, and philanthropic leaders who stand with us in supporting survivors and preserving memory through action.

  • Support for direct assistance and life-sustaining services
  • Investment in education, outreach, and community response
  • Partnerships that extend compassion to vulnerable older adults

Every partnership helps sustain care, connection, and hope.

Behind every grant, sponsorship, and community alliance is a shared commitment to stand with Holocaust survivors. The Blue Card values these relationships and the compassion they represent.

Together, we help provide essential aid, amplify awareness, and ensure survivors are supported with the respect and care they deserve.
