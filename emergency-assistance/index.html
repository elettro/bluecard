EMERGENCY ASSISTANCE

For urgent needs that affect a Holocaust survivor’s safety, stability, health, and daily living conditions.

Many Holocaust survivors continue to live with the emotional weight of displacement, loss, and insecurity. A threat to housing, health, or basic necessities can carry a deep emotional impact in addition to a financial one. Our emergency support helps survivors maintain independence with care, respect, and dignity.

For survivors living on limited income, one unexpected expense can create a serious crisis. The Blue Card provides one-time emergency grant assistance to help improve the living conditions of Holocaust survivors facing urgent hardship.

Emergency cash assistance

One-time support helps address urgent expenses so survivors can remain safe, stable, and cared for during moments of hardship.

Emergency financial assistance may include:

  • Payments to help prevent eviction
  • Homelessness prevention
  • Shut-off prevention for utilities
  • Emergency relocation
  • Home add-ins and repairs such as stair lifts or plumbing
  • Purchase and repair of essential appliances
  • Food purchases
  • Dental care
  • Medical care and medical equipment not covered by insurance
  • Essential clothing and footwear
  • Burial expenses

Natural disaster relief and crisis support

The Blue Card’s Natural Disasters and Severe Weather Fund helps provide food, vital medical care, and practical urgent relief to survivors affected by fires, hurricanes, severe weather, and other crises. From food and medical supplies to caregiver support and emergency aid, this fund helps protect vulnerable survivors during moments of crisis.

It can include:

  • Food and groceries
  • Vital medical care and supplies
  • Caregiver support
  • Financial and emotional assistance during displacement
  • Support through local volunteers, synagogues, and partner organizations

Other emergency aid

The Blue Card also responds when urgent situations fall outside standard categories but still threaten a survivor’s safety, stability, or dignity.

  • Essential clothing, appliances, and home repairs
  • Aid for Holocaust survivors facing antisemitic attacks
