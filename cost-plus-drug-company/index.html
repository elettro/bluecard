Expanding access to affordable medication

This partnership enables The Blue Card to offer Holocaust survivors nationwide access to low-cost generic medication shipped directly to their homes.

All Holocaust survivors can benefit from this program. The Blue Card also offers educational seminars on medication affordability to help survivors understand available options more clearly.

Low-cost generic medication
Helps reduce prescription expenses through more affordable access.
Direct home delivery
Medication can be shipped directly to survivors’ homes nationwide.
Nationwide reach
Available to Holocaust survivors across the country.
Educational seminars
Helps survivors learn more about medication affordability.

Why this partnership matters

Prescription affordability remains a major concern for many older adults. This partnership helps create another pathway to lower-cost medication and clearer information around access.

📦Convenient delivery
Helps reduce barriers to obtaining medication.
💲Lower-cost options

📘Better understanding

Helping survivors afford medication means helping protect health, stability, and peace of mind.
