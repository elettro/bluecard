Young Leadership

The Young Leadership Committee reflects the next generation’s commitment to honoring Holocaust survivors through action, remembrance, philanthropy, and community.

It brings together emerging leaders, advocates, and supporters who want to preserve survivor memory while helping advance The Blue Card’s mission in meaningful, visible, and practical ways.
