Why Our Work Matters

Direct Support. Preserved Dignity.

Having endured unimaginable loss and persecution during the Holocaust, many survivors continue to face hardship today. The Blue Card helps ensure that Holocaust survivors in need can live their final years with the dignity they deserve.

Since 1934, the Blue Card has been the only U.S. nonprofit dedicated solely to providing direct financial assistance to Holocaust survivors in need, helping them afford essentials like food, healthcare, and daily living support.
