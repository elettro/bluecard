TERS – Telephone Emergency Response Unit

One button. Immediate help. 24/7 protection for Holocaust survivors living independently.

Support This Program

The Blue Card’s goal is to keep Holocaust survivors in their homes and independent for as long as possible. Many survivors experience severe anxiety when faced with leaving their homes for institutional care.

The Telephone Emergency Response System (TERS) provides a life-saving solution that restores confidence, safety, and dignity.

Wearable Alert

Necklace, wrist, or home unit for immediate access to help.

One-Button Emergency

Instant connection to a trained care specialist.

24/7 Protection

Round-the-clock monitoring and response.

Family Confidence

Peace of mind for loved ones and caregivers.

Financial Support

The Blue Card provides TERS systems at no cost to survivors who cannot afford installation, service, or maintenance.

11 Years of Impact

This program has supported Holocaust survivors for over a decade, partnering with trusted emergency alert providers.

COVID-19 Response

During the pandemic, TERS enabled real-time wellness checks and rapid response for essential needs such as food, hygiene supplies, and protective equipment.
